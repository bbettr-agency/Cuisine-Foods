## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect
it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. It applies to the website
cuisinefoods.co.za and to enquiries you send us through it, by WhatsApp, by phone or by email.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you give us

When you request a bulk cooking oil quote or a used cooking oil collection, we ask for:

- your first name;
- your business name;
- your phone number;
- your email address;
- your type of business;
- the oil you need and an estimated monthly volume, or an estimate of the used oil you produce;
- your province and, where applicable, your region and area.

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we also receive whatever you choose to tell us.

We do not ask for identity numbers, payment card details or other special personal information
through this website. Please do not send them through the enquiry forms.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- **Campaign information.** If you arrive from an advert or a link, the web address may carry
  campaign details (for example `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`) and click identifiers from
  advertising platforms (for example Meta's `fbclid`). If you submit an enquiry, these are
  saved with it, together with the page you first arrived on, so that we know which advert or
  link led to the enquiry.
- **Technical information.** Our hosting provider processes standard technical data needed to
  deliver the website, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

## 6. Cookies and the Meta Pixel

**Essential storage.** The website uses your browser's storage to remember your advertising
choice (below) and, during a visit, the campaign information described in section 3.2. This is
needed for the site to work as described and does not track you across other websites.

**Meta Pixel – only if you choose "Allow".** On our quote pages we ask whether you will help
us measure our adverts. If you choose **Allow**, a small piece of code from Meta Platforms
(the "Meta Pixel") loads. It sets cookies (such as `_fbp` and `_fbc`) and tells Meta that a
page was viewed and, if you send an enquiry, that an enquiry was made. We do not send Meta
your name, email address, phone number or business name with these events. Meta may combine
this information with other information it holds about you; Meta's own privacy policy
explains how. [CONFIRM – that "Automatic advanced matching" is switched OFF in Meta Events
Manager. If it is on, Meta's code may itself collect hashed contact details from form fields
and this paragraph must be changed.]

If you choose **No thanks**, the Meta Pixel does not load and no advertising cookies are set.
Your enquiry is handled in exactly the same way.

**Changing your mind.** Clear this website's stored data in your browser settings and you
will be asked again on your next visit. [CONFIRM – whether a "change advertising choice" link

- withdraw consent you have given (this does not affect what was done before you withdrew it);
- object at any time to direct marketing;
- complain to the Information Regulator.
